# DriverLink Deep Research Plan

## Context
User has provided a detailed HTML/JS prototype for "DriverLink - Shopify for Independent Drivers" — a platform enabling independent drivers/chauffeurs to create their own branded storefronts, manage bookings, handle quotes, and operate their own service businesses independently of rideshare giants like Uber/Lyft.

## Research Objectives
Conduct multi-dimensional deep research to validate and enrich this business concept across:
1. **Market Landscape** — Independent driver/chauffeur market size, trends, and dynamics
2. **Competitive Intelligence** — Direct competitors, indirect alternatives, and platform players
3. **Business Model Analysis** — "Shopify for X" SaaS model in service industries, monetization strategies
4. **Technology & Product** — Technical architecture, feature benchmarking, best practices
5. **Regulatory & Compliance** — Insurance, licensing, and legal considerations for independent drivers
6. **Go-to-Market Strategy** — Target segments, acquisition channels, and growth tactics

## Execution Stages

### Stage 1 — Deep Research (Parallel Swarm)
- Load `deep-research-swarm` skill
- Deploy 6 parallel research agents covering the 6 dimensions above
- Cross-verify findings, collect data points, source URLs

### Stage 2 — Synthesis & Report Writing
- Load `report-writing` skill
- Compile all research into a structured strategic report
- Include actionable recommendations for the DriverLink concept

### Stage 3 — Artifact Production
- Load `docx` skill
- Convert final report to professional Word document
- Deliver to user

## Deliverables
- Comprehensive research report (Word document)
- Market data and competitive landscape
- Strategic recommendations and roadmap
