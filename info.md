# DriverLink Research Findings for Design

## Product Overview
DriverLink is a "Shopify for Independent Drivers" platform — a vertical SaaS that lets professional chauffeurs, private drivers, and car service operators create their own branded booking storefronts. Think Shopify meets StyleSeat meets Toast, purpose-built for the chauffeur industry.

## Key Features to Include in UI

### Public-Facing
- **Hero landing page** showing the platform value proposition
- **Driver directory** — browse/search/filter independent drivers by city, vehicle type, service
- **Driver profile/storefront** — branded page with photo, bio, vehicle, services, reviews, booking button
- **Booking flow** — multi-step booking with service selection, date/time, dynamic fields (flight #, luggage, etc.)
- **Pricing page** — tier comparison ($49/$99/$199)

### Driver Dashboard (authenticated view)
- **Overview/Analytics** — earnings chart, bookings count, upcoming trips
- **Booking management** — status workflow (new → accepted → confirmed → completed)
- **CRM** — customer database with profiles, notes, booking history
- **Services editor** — add/edit service templates (airport, hourly, VIP, senior, errands)
- **Storefront editor** — customize bio, photo, vehicle info, USPs
- **Earnings** — revenue breakdown, payout tracking

### Admin Dashboard
- **Driver approvals** — pending/review/approved status management
- **Platform analytics** — total drivers, bookings, revenue

## Market Data for Content
- $74.5B global chauffeur market, 70% fragmentation
- Independent chauffeurs earn $50-$175/hr vs Uber $20-$25/hr
- 25K-40K independent drivers in US
- No dominant vertical SaaS platform exists
- Corporate travel (49-58%), Airport (47%), NEMT ($4.4-6.6B) top segments
- Toast ($6.15B rev), GlossGenius ($100M ARR) validate model

## Design Direction
- Premium, professional, trustworthy — NOT Uber/Lyft gig aesthetic
- Dark luxury tones (black, deep charcoal, gold accents) — think premium chauffeur service
- Clean typography with excellent hierarchy
- Data-dense dashboards with clear visual hierarchy
- Mobile-responsive throughout
- The prototype uses Plus Jakarta Sans font with slate/blue tones

## User Personas
1. **Marcus** — Corporate chauffeur in Atlanta, Cadillac Escalade, 8 years experience, targeting executive clients
2. **Sarah** — Family/senior driver in Denver, Subaru Outback, focuses on senior care and kids transport
3. **Elena** — Eco-luxury driver in Miami, Tesla Model Y, serves corporate events and resort transfers
